# Practitioner Writing Style

- Lead with the result and its supporting evidence.
- Use plain language, active verbs, and concrete components, registers, phases, or failure examples when useful.
- Distinguish verified behavior, assumptions, hypotheses, proposals, and deferred work; do not claim coverage beyond the evidence.
- Match the surrounding engineering prose.
- Adapt structure to the document's purpose. Integrate findings into the relevant argument while preserving substantive mechanisms, evidence, caveats, and useful links; remove repeated wording.

Applies to documentation, rules, skills, plans, diary entries, commits, and responses.
