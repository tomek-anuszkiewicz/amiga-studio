# Roadmap and milestone policy

[ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) is a pending/active backlog in priority order, not
history. Remove fully implemented and verified work; no completed tags, checkboxes,
strikethrough or historical capability summaries. Partial tasks retain only remaining
scope and its evidence gate. Keep near-term work concrete and distant work concise;
group related work and make dependencies/acceptance clear. User priorities prevail;
hardware ordering follows clocks/arbitration, autonomous DMA/coprocessors, display,
peripherals and firmware integration.

[TA-organize-roadmap](../skills/TA-organize-roadmap/SKILL.md) consolidates/orders selected
pending work. [TA-prune-roadmap](../skills/TA-prune-roadmap/SKILL.md) removes selected
completed work using supplied evidence. Organization is not completion; missing
pruning evidence is reported without launching verification.

A minor roadmap point or major milestone completes only with milestone verification,
broader semantic parity, scoped compliance/compatible enrichment, checkpoint review,
diary compaction, backlog pruning and its completion commit. Preserve still-required
work. Matching broader assessment may satisfy scoped overlap; each obligation has
its separately selected operation. Pruning remains provisional until those required
results and the closure commit exist. A user-directed scope closure is recorded as
such, not as verified implementation.

Major milestone sign-off also requires stage-level documentation and instruction
governance assessment through [TA-review-docs](../skills/TA-review-docs/SKILL.md).
A matching documentation-lane report satisfies this obligation when its scope
covers the stage and its evidence applies to the sign-off candidate. A diff-only
review does not establish that broader coverage. This obligation does not add an
audit to ordinary edits or require a second assessment of already covered scope.

Verified closure extends the preparation
[storage observation](../references/agent-tooling.md#local-artifact-storage) with relevant
completed-task temporary directories and scoped cleanup recommendations. One report
serves commit/closure together; retain active diagnostics and delete nothing without
separate authorization. Do not use transient debug percentages or restore the removed
scorecard. Re-establish vAmigaTS reporting format if that verification track resumes.
