# Roadmap and Milestone Policy

- [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) contains pending and active work in priority order. Keep project descriptions in README and design specifications, execution history in diary and Git, and maintenance procedures in the companion skill.
- Remove fully implemented and verified tasks; do not retain completed tags, checked boxes, strikethrough, or historical capability summaries. For partially implemented tasks, retain only the remaining scope and its verification gate.
- Keep near-term tasks concrete and distant work concise. Group related work, state dependencies and acceptance evidence, and renumber coherently after reordering. User-directed priorities take precedence; hardware implementation follows clocks and bus arbitration, autonomous DMA/coprocessors, video serialization, peripherals, then firmware integration.
- A minor roadmap point or major milestone completes only after the milestone gate, [semantic parity review](../skills/audit-semantic-parity/SKILL.md), affected design synchronization and checkpoint review, diary compaction, backlog pruning, and a commit recording that completion. Reorganizing the roadmap does not itself complete its planned implementation milestones.
- Use [roadmap-maintenance](../skills/roadmap-maintenance/SKILL.md) for evidence review, consolidation, pruning, and reference checks; [docs-maintenance.md](docs-maintenance.md) and [diary-maintenance.md](diary-maintenance.md) own their respective obligations.
- Keep transient debug percentages out of the roadmap. Re-establish the vAmigaTS reporting format when that verification track resumes; do not require the removed scorecard.
