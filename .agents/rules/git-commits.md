# Batch authority and readiness

## Readiness Before Freeze

Use the root [authority and checkout choice](../../AGENTS.md#choose-the-task-checkout).
The user selects staging, commits, publication, cleanup and push. Reuse concrete
authorization for routine corrections and preserve unrelated index entries.
Apply the narrow [integration exceptions](git-merge-commits.md) only to their
supplied source, target and evidence. The [completion criteria](../../Obsidian/Amiga/Design/Verification/Testing%20Strategy%20and%20Quality%20Assurance.md#roadmap-and-milestone-completion)
require a closure commit at actual roadmap and milestone triggers.

One complete candidate supplies preparation, all reviews, canonical verification
and the mechanical commits. Identify all intended staged, unstaged and new
paths or hunks, exact baseline and commit partition. Verify the selected working
tree against ordinary HEAD or the recorded immutable integration base. Keep
unrelated content out of the candidate and preserve originally staged versions.

- [Preparation](../skills/TA-prepare-batch/SKILL.md) owns the partition, messages,
  diary and storage observation. Resolve required specification, documentation
  and changed-code coverage gaps before freeze.
- Gather four fresh independent [lane reviews](../references/review-common.md)
  covering behavior, architecture, tests and evidence, and documentation.
  Each approval retains its exact scope and evidence through corrections.
  The [review continuation](../references/review-common.md#review-after-correction)
  owns focused checks, remaining approvals and the human checkpoint.
- Require passing [canonical commit verification](../skills/TA-verify-batch/SKILL.md)
  for the complete candidate and [milestone verification](../skills/TA-verify-milestone/SKILL.md)
  at its actual trigger. Any failed, missing or invalidated required gate blocks
  readiness. Share only isolated resources when checks and reviews overlap.
- [Mechanical commit](../skills/TA-commit-batch/SKILL.md) freezes the accepted
  candidate, partition, messages and evidence, then creates and checks the
  authorized sequence. [Publication](../skills/TA-close-task/SKILL.md) and
  [cleanup](../skills/TA-cleanup-task/SKILL.md) consume their own prerequisites.

The [capture guide](../../tools/workflow/capture/README.md#starting-inputs)
owns approved input versions. The [post-publication language policy](language-policy.md)
owns the final scan and the vocabulary-only closure commit.
