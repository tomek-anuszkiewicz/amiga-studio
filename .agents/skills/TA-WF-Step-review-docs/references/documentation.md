# Specifications, documentation and governance

Apply the shared [documentation criteria](../../../references/documentation-criteria.md)
and relevant instruction ownership/specification requirements. They include scoped
compliance selection, broader milestone obligations and document-value assessment;
using these criteria does not invoke another public skill.
Apply the shared [prose and narrative criteria](../../../references/narrative-criteria.md)
according to the document's purpose; preserve unique evidence and reasoning when
assessing reorganized articles or other documentation. Review approved
requirements and retained source-backed knowledge in both directions under the
shared scope rules; stage-wide assessments include unchanged affected material.

Trace changed requirements into affected code/tests even without an implementation
diff. Assess required supplied audits and their coverage rather than repeating them.
Classify descriptive versus requirement-changing corrections and identify any other
lanes affected by changed criteria or evidence. For changed instructions, walk the
real invocation and inspect ownership, activation, stopping points and active callers.

Check metadata/link semantics with the full Design audit included in this skill,
and assess triggered diary/roadmap obligations. Preserve
hardware knowledge, independent requirements and unique rationale. Report needed
enrichment or repairs; do not edit documents, index content or launch unrelated audits.

Check that explanations are concise, evidence-bounded and in the user's language.
Assess the small shared result format and actual selected operation, without
requiring a transition matrix, fixed review lanes or a lifecycle record.
For instruction changes, verify that active callers no longer invoke removed
commands and that remaining operational requirements have one authoritative owner.
